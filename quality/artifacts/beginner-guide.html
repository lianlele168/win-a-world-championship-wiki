Win A World Championship Beginner Guide

The game rewards patient team building. Your first goal is not a perfect squad; it is a playable core that turns free code rewards into tournament progress.

01

Redeem current codes first

Codes give the fastest early budget. Use the current Roblox-listed codes before judging your first squad.

Open guide
02

Open packs for a basic spine

Aim for one scorer, one midfielder, and one defender before spending rerolls on fine-tuning.

Open guide
03

Use rerolls only on keepers

Team and Year Rerolls are more valuable on cards you plan to keep through multiple tournaments.

Open guide
04

Enter cups with a balanced shape

Pick a formation that covers your weakest line instead of forcing an all-attack setup too early.

Open guide

Beginner mistakes to avoid

Spending rerolls before you know which cards are keepers.
Opening packs for fame instead of fixing the weakest line.
Entering cups with no reroll reserve left.
