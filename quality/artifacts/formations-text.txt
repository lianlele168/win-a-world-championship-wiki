FORMATION FIT GUIDE
Win A World Championship Formations

Pick your formation from the cards you have, not the cards you wish you had. These setups focus on role fit and tournament stability.

4-3-3

Elite finisher plus pace winger

Wide pressure
Fast transitions
Easy scoring outlets

Watch out: Needs midfield quality or it becomes too open.

4-2-3-1

Balanced tournament runs

Midfield protection
One clear striker
Good weak-line coverage

Watch out: Can feel slow if your attacking midfielder is weak.

4-4-2

Two good attackers but limited midfield stars

Simple structure
Direct chances
Good for early squads

Watch out: Harder to dominate possession against stronger teams.

5-3-2

Protecting a lead or underdog runs

Defensive stability
Safer finals
Reduces weak defender exposure

Watch out: Needs two attackers who can create without many chances.
