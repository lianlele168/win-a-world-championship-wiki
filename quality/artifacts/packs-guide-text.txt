COIN SPEND PLANNING
Win A World Championship Packs Guide

Packs are how you create new upgrade chances, but rerolls decide whether good pulls become tournament-ready. Spend coins in cycles instead of dumping your full bank.

BEGINNER
First Core Pack Cycle

Find one scoring outlet and one midfield connector before spending rerolls aggressively.

Use codes first, then open only enough packs to replace clear D-tier fillers.

Overspending early leaves no coins for the next update or pack cost jump.

MID GAME
Weakest Line Upgrade

Pick the line with the lowest real match impact: attack, midfield, defense, or keeper.

Spend pack coins only when the new card can solve that line; otherwise save rerolls.

Chasing a famous card while ignoring your weakest line creates unbalanced teams.

TOURNAMENT PUSH
Nation and Year Alignment

Use team and year rerolls to improve fit after your main card roles are stable.

Do not burn year rerolls on filler cards; spend them on S/A-tier keepers.

Perfect synergy on weak cards is worse than strong cards with acceptable fit.

LATE GAME
Finals Bankroll

Enter tough cups with enough rerolls left to fix one unlucky draft slot.

Keep a small reserve rather than spending every code reward immediately.

A zero-reserve squad can get stuck with a bad year or nation before finals.
