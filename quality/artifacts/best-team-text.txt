TOURNAMENT SQUAD FRAMEWORK
Win A World Championship Best Team Guide

A strong team is not just a collection of famous cards. Build a balanced spine first, then use rerolls to align nation and year around cards worth keeping.

1. Reliable scorer

Your first tournament-ready card should create goals. Without a scorer, extra defenders only slow losses down.

2. Control midfielder

Midfield keeps the squad stable across nation and year changes, especially before your card pool is deep.

3. Defensive anchor

Once you can score, stop cheap goals with a center back or defensive midfielder that fits your shape.

4. Keeper and connectors

Upgrade keeper and fullbacks after the core is solved, then improve nation-year fit with rerolls.
